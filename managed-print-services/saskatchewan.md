# Managed Print Services in Saskatchewan

Managed print services (MPS) can help organizations manage printers, copiers, supplies, service and print-related costs through a coordinated program.

A useful MPS review should look at:

- Number and type of devices
- Monthly page volume
- Black-and-white versus colour usage
- Toner consumption
- Service history
- Device age
- User and department requirements
- Network and scanning requirements

See the [managed print services overview](https://vonex.ca/managed-print) and [Kyocera managed print services in Saskatoon](https://vonex.ca/kyocera-managed-print-services-saskatoon).

Organizations can also review the [printer maintenance contract information](https://vonex.ca/printer-maintenance-contract-saskatoon) when comparing service arrangements.
