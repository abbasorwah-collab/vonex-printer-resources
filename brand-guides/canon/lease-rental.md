# Canon Printer & Copier Lease and Rental Guide

Compare Canon equipment by print volume, colour needs, scanning, paper handling, finishing, supplies, service coverage and contract terms—not just the monthly payment.

See the [Vonex lease and rental guide](https://vonex.ca/lease-rental) for general lease considerations.

Before signing, calculate expected equipment, service, supply and financing costs across the full term. Also confirm what happens at the end of the agreement.

For local equipment information, see [Vonex office equipment](https://vonex.ca/office-equipment).
