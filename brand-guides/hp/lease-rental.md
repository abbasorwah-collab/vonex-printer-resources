# HP Printer Lease and Rental Guide

For an HP business printer, compare expected volume, colour requirements, scanning, supplies, service coverage, warranty terms and contract length.

See the [Vonex lease and rental guide](https://vonex.ca/lease-rental) for general lease considerations.

Before signing, compare the total expected equipment, service and supply cost rather than only the monthly payment.

For HP service information in Saskatoon, see [Vonex HP printer service](https://vonex.ca/hp-printer-service-saskatoon).
