# Konica Minolta Printer & Copier Lease and Rental Guide

Businesses considering a Konica Minolta printer or multifunction copier should compare equipment, service, supplies and contract terms—not only the monthly payment.

## What to compare
- Print and copy volume
- Colour versus black-and-white requirements
- Paper capacity and sizes
- Scanning and workflow requirements
- Finishing options
- Toner and supply costs
- Preventive maintenance and repair coverage
- Contract length and end-of-term terms

For general lease and rental information, see the [Vonex lease and rental guide](https://vonex.ca/lease-rental).

Before signing, compare the complete expected cost over the contract term, including equipment, service, supplies and any volume charges.

For local office equipment information, see [Vonex office equipment](https://vonex.ca/office-equipment).
