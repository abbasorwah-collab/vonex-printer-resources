# Vonex Offsite SEO Engine

## Goal
Build legitimate offsite authority for both Vonex local/service pages and Shop.Vonex.ca ecommerce pages, while connecting offsite activity to leads and sales.

## 1. Citation cleanup
Maintain one canonical business identity:
- Vonex Business Solutions
- Unit 400 – 111 2nd Avenue South, Saskatoon, SK S7K 1K6
- 306-881-0341
- https://vonex.ca/

Prioritize correction of stale/incorrect listings before creating new ones. Never create duplicate business profiles.

## 2. Local authority
Prioritize relevant Saskatchewan and Canadian business resources, chambers/associations, supplier/dealer directories, technology directories, and local business publications.

Target topics:
- printer/copier sales and service
- commercial copier lease/rental
- printer lease/rental
- managed print services
- office equipment
- printer repair

Use natural branded/descriptive links. Do not force exact-match anchors.

## 3. Resource-link outreach
Create useful assets that other sites have a reason to reference:
- business printer maintenance checklist
- copier lease vs rental guide
- commercial printer cost calculator
- managed print assessment checklist
- printer downtime cost worksheet
- toner cost-per-page guide
- Saskatchewan office printer buying guide

Outreach should be to sites where the resource genuinely improves an existing page.

## 4. Brand/manufacturer ecosystem
Look for legitimate dealer/reseller/service locators, partner pages, supplier directories, training/event pages, and customer-resource pages where Vonex qualifies.

Only claim an authorization or partnership when current and verified.

## 5. Local PR and partnerships
Track:
- customer case studies (with permission)
- local business collaborations
- community sponsorships
- trade events
- supplier announcements
- new service/territory announcements

Prefer editorial mentions that are earned because of a real event, partnership, or useful information.

## 6. Unlinked mention recovery
Search for existing mentions of Vonex, vonex.ca, and relevant business references. Where a legitimate mention lacks a link, request a link to the most relevant page.

## 7. Broken-link/resource replacement
Find relevant Canadian printer, office-equipment, business-technology, and procurement resources with broken/outdated links. Offer a genuinely useful Vonex resource as a replacement only when it matches the original intent.

## 8. Shop.Vonex.ca offsite SEO
Treat the ecommerce site separately from the local service site.

Rotate targets:
- toner
- ink
- printer supplies
- office supplies
- Brother
- HP
- Canon
- Lexmark
- Zebra
- printer buying guides
- individual priority products

Do not represent Shop.Vonex.ca as a separate physical location.

## 9. Product-page authority
For individual products, prioritize links from:
- useful compatibility guides
- office-equipment buying guides
- procurement/resource pages
- business technology articles
- manufacturer-compatible resource discussions
- relevant customer education pages

Avoid mass product-directory submissions and thin affiliate pages.

## 10. Weekly operating system
Every week:
1. Find new relevant prospects.
2. Check whether Vonex already has a listing/mention.
3. Check domain/page relevance and quality.
4. Choose the most relevant Vonex target URL.
5. Draft a natural outreach angle.
6. Record status in the backlink tracker.
7. Verify approved/live links.
8. Record referral traffic or leads when measurable.

## 11. Quality rules
Never use:
- automated mass submissions
- fake profiles
- comment spam
- forum spam
- PBNs
- paid links solely for ranking
- irrelevant directories
- duplicate business listings
- exact-match anchor campaigns

Success is measured by relevant referring domains, qualified referral traffic, branded search growth, ranking improvements, calls, quote requests, and sales—not backlink count alone.
