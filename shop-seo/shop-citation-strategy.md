# Shop.vonex.ca — Canadian Ecommerce Citation & Link-Building Plan

This guide is for the national ecommerce store at https://shop.vonex.ca/.

## Keep the two websites separate

- **vonex.ca** — local office equipment sales, leasing, managed print and onsite service in Saskatchewan.
- **shop.vonex.ca** — Canada-wide ecommerce for toner, ink, printers, printer supplies and office supplies.

For ecommerce citations, use the shop URL where the listing specifically supports an online store or product website. Do not replace the main Vonex local-business website with the shop URL on local listings.

## Recommended business description

> Vonex is a Canadian online supplier of toner, ink cartridges, printers, printer supplies and office supplies, serving businesses across Canada.

Use only claims that remain accurate for the store.

## Do not create low-quality duplicate listings

Do not create multiple listings on the same directory, fake city locations, fake storefront addresses, or pages solely for backlinks.

For national ecommerce SEO, relevance is more important than the number of directory links.

## Anchor-text guidance

Rotate natural wording:

- Vonex online store
- Canadian printer supplies
- printer supplies
- toner and ink
- business printer supplies

Avoid repeatedly using one exact-match phrase such as "cheap toner Canada."

## Existing Canadian opportunities

Canadian Business Directory accepts Canadian business submissions and provides website, category, description and keyword fields. Check for an existing Vonex listing before creating one. 

The Canadian Business Directory has an Office Furniture and Equipment category that includes office-supply and printer-related businesses.

Spott.ca provides Canadian business profiles and marketplace/business discovery.

ZipLeaf Canada has printer and office-equipment product categories and already indexes Vonex-related printer/office-equipment information.

## NAP caution

For the ecommerce store, do not invent a national physical location.

If a directory requires a physical address, use the real Vonex business information only when appropriate. If the directory is specifically for ecommerce businesses and allows a website-only listing, use the shop website.

## Quality rule

Every new citation should pass at least one of these tests:

- Relevant to Canadian businesses
- Relevant to office equipment or printer supplies
- Relevant to ecommerce
- Relevant to a manufacturer/industry relationship
- A legitimate local or industry organization
- A useful resource that can naturally reference Vonex

Avoid automated backlink networks, spun articles, fake profiles, paid link farms and unrelated directories.
