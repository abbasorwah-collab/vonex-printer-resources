# Shop.vonex.ca Link-Building Plan

## Purpose
Build legitimate authority for the Canada-wide ecommerce site without treating it as a separate physical location.

## Priority link targets
1. Shop homepage — https://shop.vonex.ca/
2. Toner collection — use the current live Shopify toner URL
3. Ink collection — use the current live Shopify ink URL
4. Printer supplies collection
5. Office supplies collection
6. Brother-related resource/category page
7. HP-related resource/category page
8. Canon-related resource/category page
9. Lexmark-related resource/category page
10. Zebra-related resource/category page
11. Educational printer/toner buying guides

## Link sources to pursue
- Relevant Canadian business directories: legitimate directories only; check for an existing Vonex listing first.
- Printer and office-equipment resources: Canadian or industry-specific pages that genuinely help businesses choose printers, toner, ink, labels or office equipment.
- Educational content: toner identification, toner vs ink, business printer supply checklist, maintenance checklist, label printer selection, printer cost calculator, compatible vs OEM toner.
- Digital PR/outreach: useful data, guides, checklists or calculators for business publications, associations and technology resources.
- Broken-link opportunities: relevant printer/office-supply resources containing dead links where a Vonex resource is a genuine replacement.

## Anchor-text policy
Keep anchors natural and varied: Vonex; Vonex Business Solutions; Vonex printer supplies; printer supplies; toner and ink; business printer supplies; visit the printer supplies store.
Avoid building a large percentage of links with one exact-match commercial phrase.

## Important separation
vonex.ca is the local Saskatchewan office-equipment/service site.
shop.vonex.ca is the Canada-wide ecommerce site.
Do not create fake locations or duplicate business profiles for the shop. Where a directory only supports one Vonex company listing, evaluate whether the existing listing should remain pointed to the primary company website rather than creating another profile.

## Workflow
1. Research opportunity.
2. Check whether Vonex already has a listing/link.
3. Confirm topical relevance and quality.
4. Select the most relevant shop target page.
5. Record the opportunity in external-backlink-opportunities.csv.
6. Submit or contact manually.
7. Record the outcome.
8. Verify the link after publication.
