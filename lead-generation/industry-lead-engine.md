# Vonex.ca Industry Lead Engine

## Scope
This engine is exclusively for Vonex.ca business leads. shop.vonex.ca is excluded.

## Priority industries
1. Medical clinics
2. Pharmacies
3. Law offices
4. Industrial businesses
5. Eligible non-profit organizations

## Industry offer framework
Each industry campaign should combine:
- printer/copier sales
- leasing/rental
- onsite service and repair
- maintenance
- toner/supplies programs
- managed print where appropriate
- free print/equipment assessment
- clear industry-specific CTA

Do not make unsupported claims about privacy, security, regulatory compliance or specialized equipment. Verify each claim before publishing.

## Medical clinics
Lead around:
- reliable office printing
- multifunction printers and scanning
- document workflows
- equipment service
- toner/supplies
- leasing/replacement
- multi-device environments

CTA: Request a Medical Office Print Assessment.

## Pharmacies
Lead around:
- office and document printers
- label/thermal printing where appropriate
- barcode/label workflows where applicable
- equipment service
- supplies
- replacement and leasing

CTA: Ask About Pharmacy Printer & Label Solutions.

## Law offices
Lead around:
- high-volume document printing
- scanning and copying
- MFPs
- service/maintenance
- leasing
- toner management
- workflow efficiency

CTA: Request a Law Office Print Assessment.

## Industrial businesses
Lead around:
- Zebra/barcode/label printing
- warehouse and shipping workflows
- thermal printing
- office MFPs
- service and maintenance
- supplies
- replacement planning

CTA: Request an Industrial Printing Assessment.

## Non-profits
Vonex has special pricing for eligible non-profit organizations. Use:
- special non-profit pricing
- printer/copier sales
- leasing/rental
- service and repair
- toner/supplies
- managed print where appropriate
- free assessment

CTA: Get Non-Profit Pricing.

## Industry + brand matrix
Medical: Kyocera, Xerox, Konica Minolta, HP and other verified supported equipment.
Pharmacy: Zebra plus office printer/MFP brands where appropriate.
Law: Xerox, Kyocera, Konica Minolta and other verified supported equipment.
Industrial: Zebra plus office printer/MFP brands where appropriate.
Non-profit: equipment selected by print volume, workflow and budget.

This is a planning matrix, not a claim that every brand is formally authorized for every service.

## Outreach
For each industry, build a prospect list with:
organization, city, contact, phone, email, industry, current equipment if known, estimated print need, lead status, next follow-up.

Sources should be legitimate business directories, chambers/associations, public organization websites and referrals. Avoid purchased spam lists, deceptive outreach and fabricated leads.

## Funnel
Industry search/outreach -> industry page -> call/form -> qualification -> assessment -> quote -> sale/service agreement -> revenue.

## Measurement
Track:
industry -> brand/model -> city -> source -> lead -> quote -> won/lost -> revenue.
