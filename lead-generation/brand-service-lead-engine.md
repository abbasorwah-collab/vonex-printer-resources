# Vonex.ca Brand Service Lead Engine

## Purpose
Generate qualified service, repair, maintenance, replacement and equipment leads for Vonex.ca. This system applies only to vonex.ca, not shop.vonex.ca.

## Brand clusters
1. Zebra — label, barcode and thermal printer service
2. Xerox — printer, copier and MFP service
3. Kyocera — printer, copier, TASKalfa and ECOSYS service
4. Konica Minolta — bizhub, copier and MFP service

## Search-to-lead architecture
Brand -> service -> problem -> model -> industry -> genuine service area -> request service.

### Core service intent
- [brand] printer repair
- [brand] printer service
- [brand] copier repair
- [brand] copier service
- [brand] MFP service
- [brand] onsite service
- [brand] maintenance
- [brand] troubleshooting
- [brand] printer not printing
- [brand] paper jam
- [brand] print quality problem
- [brand] scan problem where applicable
- [brand] network/connection problem where applicable

### Model layer
Create model pages only for models Vonex genuinely services and whose facts are verified. Each model page should cover:
- equipment type
- common service needs
- symptoms/problems
- service options
- onsite availability
- compatible Vonex services
- relevant industries
- genuine service areas
- request-service CTA

Do not invent compatibility, certification, authorization, parts availability, response times or model-specific technical facts.

## Conversion system
Every service page should have:
- Call Vonex: 306-881-0341
- Request Service
- Request a Free Assessment
- short service-request form
- equipment brand/model field
- problem/symptom field
- city field
- preferred contact method

## Brand-specific positioning
### Zebra
Focus on barcode, label, thermal and warehouse/industrial printing. Target repair, calibration, print quality, labels/ribbons, connectivity and model-specific service where verified.

### Xerox
Focus on business printers, copiers and MFPs. Target repair, preventive maintenance, scanning, print quality, paper-feed issues and model-specific service where verified.

### Kyocera
Focus on business printers, copiers, TASKalfa and ECOSYS equipment. Target service, maintenance, repair, scanning, print quality and model-specific support where verified.

### Konica Minolta
Focus on bizhub and other business MFP/copier equipment. Target repair, preventive maintenance, scanning, paper-feed and print-quality issues and verified model pages.

## Location strategy
Primary: Saskatoon.
Secondary growth market: Regina.
Then genuine Saskatchewan service markets such as Prince Albert, Moose Jaw, Yorkton, North Battleford, Warman and Martensville where service coverage is real.

Do not create fake offices or duplicate thin city pages.

## Industry crossovers
Prioritize:
- Medical clinics
- Pharmacies
- Law offices
- Industrial businesses
- Non-profit organizations

Examples:
- Zebra + industrial
- Zebra + pharmacy
- Xerox + law office
- Kyocera + medical clinic
- Konica Minolta + law office

Only build crossover pages when the page can provide genuinely useful, unique information.

## Competitive strategy
Compete through useful search coverage and conversion depth, not unsupported superiority claims:
- brand service pages
- problem/troubleshooting resources
- verified model pages
- industry-specific service pages
- local service pages
- clear CTAs
- measurable lead attribution

## KPIs
Track:
brand -> model -> problem -> city -> lead source -> quote -> service job -> revenue.

Primary outcomes:
qualified leads, service jobs, quotes, equipment opportunities and revenue.
