# Sharp Copier Guide

Sharp multifunction copiers can be used for high-volume office printing, copying and scanning.

When replacing an older copier, document the existing monthly volume, paper sizes, finishing requirements, scan destinations and service history. These details make equipment comparisons more useful.

See Vonex's [copier service information](https://vonex.ca/copier-service-saskatoon) and [office equipment resources](https://vonex.ca/office-equipment).
