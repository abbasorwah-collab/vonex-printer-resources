# Stack Measurement

Measure business outcomes as well as SEO visibility.

## Primary KPIs
- phone calls
- quote requests
- service requests
- qualified leads
- lease/rental opportunities
- ecommerce transactions
- revenue

## SEO/supporting KPIs
- impressions
- clicks
- indexed pages
- ranking visibility
- referring domains
- verified live links
- image search visibility
- page speed/image weight
- conversion rate by landing page

Do not treat a new backlink, profile, or indexed image as a success by itself. The stack exists to improve useful discovery and conversion.
