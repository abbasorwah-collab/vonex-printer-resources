# Vonex SEO Stacking Engine

## Purpose
Build connected, legitimate authority around high-intent Vonex services without creating spam networks or artificial properties.

## Stack model
Each priority service should have as many relevant layers as genuinely available:

1. Google Business Profile
2. Primary Vonex service/location page
3. Supporting Vonex article or guide
4. Internal links from related pages
5. Original image/photo asset
6. Video or YouTube asset when useful
7. Relevant business/industry citation
8. Manufacturer or industry ecosystem reference where appropriate
9. Genuine social/entity profile
10. Genuine customer review referencing the experience naturally
11. Relevant third-party editorial/resource link
12. Lead/call tracking

## Quality rules
- No fake businesses or locations.
- No fake reviews.
- No automated mass profile creation.
- No PBNs or link farms.
- No comment/forum spam.
- No paid links solely for ranking.
- No duplicate business listings.
- No fabricated GPS/EXIF data.
- No keyword-stuffed reviews or image metadata.
- Use natural, useful links and accurate business information.
- Keep vonex.ca focused on local Saskatchewan services and shop.vonex.ca focused on Canada-wide ecommerce.

## Operating cadence
Prioritize the highest commercial-intent stacks first. Complete and verify one layer before adding another weak layer. Measure calls, quote requests, leads and sales alongside rankings and links.
