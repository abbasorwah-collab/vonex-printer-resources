# Vonex Stack Playbooks

These are implementation blueprints. They do not claim that an asset exists until it is actually published and verified.

## 1. Commercial Copier Lease / Rental
Primary: https://vonex.ca/lease-rental
Support:
- copier lease vs rental guide
- copier lease buyout guide
- cost-per-page calculator
- original installation/equipment photos
- relevant local business/industry listings
- internal links from office equipment, managed print and industry pages
- genuine customer experience/review where available
- call and quote tracking

## 2. Printer & Copier Repair

- printer maintenance guide
- emergency printer repair guide

- relevant industry resource outreach

## 3. Managed Print Services

## 4. Brand Service Stacks
Use only accurate authorization/dealer language. Each brand stack can include:
- brand/service page
- equipment photos
- service/maintenance guide
- relevant manufacturer ecosystem reference
- internal links to repair, leasing and supplies
- genuine customer proof
- relevant external resources

Priority brands: Kyocera, HP, Lexmark, Zebra, Xerox, Brother, Canon, Konica Minolta, Sharp, Toshiba.

## 5. Industry Stacks
Create useful pages/content for:
- medical clinics
- dental offices
- law firms
- accounting firms
- construction
- property management
- manufacturing
- schools
- government

Each should explain actual printing needs, equipment considerations, service/uptime concerns and relevant Vonex solutions. Avoid thin city/industry doorway pages.

## 6. Shop.vonex.ca Ecommerce Stacks
Build separately from local SEO:
- toner collections
- ink collections
- printer supplies
- office supplies
- brand collections
- high-value product pages
- buying guides
- compatibility resources
