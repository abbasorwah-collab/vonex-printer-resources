# Shop.vonex.ca Product Keyword Engine

Updated: 2026-09-30

## Purpose

Build organic search visibility for individual Shop.vonex.ca product pages by matching each product to the search terms buyers actually use.

The engine covers:
- toner cartridges
- ink cartridges
- printers
- label printers
- printer supplies
- office supplies
- janitorial supplies
- networking products
- other relevant ecommerce products

The objective is qualified organic traffic and sales, not keyword stuffing.

## Keyword hierarchy

Each important product should have:

1. Primary keyword
2. Secondary keywords
3. Brand/model terms
4. Compatibility/model terms
5. Problem/use-case terms where relevant
6. Commercial intent terms
7. Related product/category terms

### Example

Product:
Brother TN920 Black Toner

Primary:
- Brother TN920 toner

Secondary:
- Brother TN920 black toner
- TN920 toner cartridge
- TN920 black toner cartridge

Compatibility:
- Brother HL-L8360CDW toner
- Brother MFC-L8900CDW toner

Commercial variations:
- buy Brother TN920 toner
- Brother TN920 toner Canada

Do not force every variation onto the page. Use only terms that accurately describe the product.

## Product page placement

Where appropriate, the primary keyword should be reflected naturally in:

- SEO title
- H1/product title
- first product-description section
- product specifications
- compatibility section
- image alt text when descriptive
- FAQ questions/answers
- related-product links
- collection/category links
- structured data fields supported by the platform

Avoid:
- keyword repetition
- hidden keywords
- unnatural city lists
- unrelated search terms
- misleading compatibility claims

## Search intent

Classify each keyword:

- transactional: user appears ready to purchase
- commercial: comparing or evaluating products
- informational: learning/research
- compatibility: checking whether a product works with a machine
- replacement: looking for a replacement cartridge/part
- troubleshooting: solving a product-related problem

Product pages should primarily target transactional and compatibility searches.

Informational searches should normally be handled by useful guides that link to the relevant product.

→ Product family

Brand

## Canada-wide city signals

Shop.vonex.ca is Canada-wide ecommerce.

Do not add city names to every product title.

Use geographic terms only when they are genuinely useful and supported by the page:

- Canada
- Canadian shipping
- major Canadian markets
- province/city buying guides where unique content exists

Do not create hundreds of duplicate product pages such as:
- Product Toronto
- Product Vancouver
- Product Calgary

Instead, build national product authority and selective province/city resources.

## Search Console opportunity engine

When Search Console data is available, prioritize products with:

### Tier 1
- meaningful impressions
- position roughly 4–20
- relevant commercial query
- low/moderate CTR
- page already indexed

### Tier 2
- meaningful impressions
- position roughly 21–40
- strong product/query match
- clear content improvement opportunity

### Tier 3
- low impressions but highly relevant commercial terms
- important strategic product
- strong internal-link opportunity

Do not optimize solely because a keyword has a high volume estimate. Relevance and conversion intent matter.

For each selected product:

## Daily rotation

The product keyword engine is part of the daily SEO rotation.

Each product pass should select a new batch rather than repeatedly reviewing the same products.

Suggested order:

1. High-impression products
2. High-intent toner/ink products
3. Major brand products
4. Printer/label-printer models
5. Products with weak titles
6. Products with weak descriptions
7. Products with compatibility opportunities
8. Long-tail products
9. New products
10. Previously optimized products for measurement

After a catalog section is completed, move to the next section.

## Important rule

Never invent compatibility, yield, specifications, part numbers or manufacturer relationships.

Product SEO must remain factually accurate.

## Success measurement

Primary:
- organic product clicks
- product-page conversions
- revenue
- qualified purchase traffic

Secondary:
- impressions
- CTR
- average position
- number of relevant queries
- indexed product pages
- internal links

The goal is to turn product pages into useful search destinations that also convert, not simply to increase keyword counts.
