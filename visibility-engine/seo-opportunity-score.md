# SEO Opportunity Score

Use a 0–100 score to prioritize work, not to judge pages as good or bad.

Suggested weighting:
- Search visibility/opportunity: 25
- Commercial intent: 20
- Business value/margin: 20
- Conversion potential: 15
- Content improvement opportunity: 10
- Strategic brand/model importance: 10

## Inputs
Use real data where available:
- Search Console
- Shopify sales/conversion data
- inventory
- margin/business value
- product/collection importance

## Rules
A high score means the opportunity deserves earlier review. It does not guarantee ranking or sales.

Do not fabricate missing metrics. Mark unavailable inputs as unknown and avoid pretending the score is precise.
