# Saskatchewan Small-Town SEO Engine

## Purpose
Expand Vonex's legitimate local search coverage beyond Saskatoon and Regina into Saskatchewan communities where Vonex genuinely provides or can provide service.

## Strategy
Town → service hub → relevant brand → relevant model → industry → CTA.

Do not create a page for every community automatically. A town page requires genuine service relevance and useful local information.

- Yorkton

## Page types
### Town hub
Example: Printer & Copier Service in Humboldt, Saskatchewan

Include only services genuinely offered:
- printer repair
- copier/MFP repair
- printer/copier sales
- leasing/rental
- managed print
- toner/supplies
- equipment replacement

## URL architecture
Prefer simple, non-duplicative structures such as:
- /printer-repair-humboldt
- /copier-service-humboldt
- /printer-repair-melfort

Do not create both a town page and a brand-town page unless the brand page has enough unique service information and search value.

## Small-town content requirements
Every town page should contain:
1. What Vonex services in the area
2. Types of businesses supported
3. Printer/copier problems addressed
4. Equipment/lease options where genuinely available
5. Relevant brands
6. Service-area context
7. Internal links to Saskatchewan and brand hubs
8. Clear call/quote CTA
9. Genuine local imagery where available

Avoid generic text swaps such as replacing one town name with another. Each page needs unique local/business context.

## Local authority
For each priority town:
- local chamber/business association
- local business directory
- economic development organization
- community organization
- relevant industry association
- genuine customer/business partnerships
- local sponsorships/events where applicable

Never create fake local addresses or duplicate Google Business Profiles.

## Measurement
Track each town separately:
- impressions
- clicks
- indexed status
- phone calls
- quote requests
- service requests
- leasing opportunities
- revenue

## Rollout
Phase 1: Tier 1 towns.
Phase 2: Tier 2 towns.
Phase 3: smaller communities only where service history, customer demand, or strong commercial relevance justifies a page.

## Important
Population is only a prioritization signal. Service coverage must be verified before publishing a location-specific claim.
