# Vonex Calculator & Tool Roadmap

Build useful tools that can earn links, support SEO, and help sales conversations. These are specifications only until implemented on the website.

## Priority 1 — Cost Per Page Calculator
Inputs:
- device model
- toner yield
- toner price
- colour/black-and-white mix
- monthly pages

Outputs:
- estimated cost per page
- monthly toner cost
- annual toner cost
- comparison between current and proposed setup

CTA: Request a free print assessment.

## Priority 2 — Copier Lease Payment Estimator
Inputs:
- equipment price
- term
- estimated financing rate
- monthly service/print costs where applicable

Outputs:
- estimated monthly equipment payment
- estimated total equipment cost

Clearly label results as estimates and require a quote for actual pricing.

## Priority 3 — Printer Replacement Calculator
Inputs:
- current device age
- monthly volume
- downtime/service cost
- toner spend
- repair spend

Outputs:
- replacement consideration indicators
- estimated annual operating cost
- assessment CTA

## Priority 4 — Toner Savings Calculator
For business customers comparing current toner spend against a monitored/MPS approach.

## SEO requirements
- Give each tool a crawlable landing page.
- Explain the methodology and assumptions.
- Add FAQ content.
- Link to relevant Vonex service pages.
- Make results shareable where practical.
- Do not publish misleading savings claims.
