# Vonex Review Engine

## Goal
Create a consistent process for requesting genuine customer feedback after completed sales, installations, repairs and service visits.

## Workflow
1. Identify completed customer interaction.
2. Wait until the customer has had a reasonable opportunity to evaluate the service.
3. Send a simple review request.
4. Provide the official review destination.
5. Do not ask customers to use specific keywords.
6. Do not offer incentives for positive reviews.
7. Do not create, edit, or submit reviews on behalf of customers.
8. Log request date and outcome without manipulating the review content.

## Suggested triggers
- repair completed
- copier/printer installation completed
- lease/rental installation completed
- managed print onboarding completed
- successful service follow-up

## Measurement
Track:
- requests sent
- reviews received
- average response rate
- review themes
- service issues requiring follow-up

A negative review should trigger customer-service follow-up, not removal or suppression.
