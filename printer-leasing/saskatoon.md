# Printer Leasing in Saskatoon

Businesses often compare buying a printer with leasing when they want predictable equipment costs, service coverage and the ability to replace equipment as needs change.

Before choosing a lease, review expected monthly print volume, colour requirements, paper sizes, finishing, scanning, service response, consumables and the total cost over the term.

For a local starting point, see Vonex's [printer lease and rental information](https://vonex.ca/lease-rental) and [office equipment options](https://vonex.ca/office-equipment).

For service considerations, see the [printer maintenance contract information for Saskatoon](https://vonex.ca/printer-maintenance-contract-saskatoon).

## Questions to ask

1. What is included in the monthly payment?
2. Is toner included?
3. What are the black-and-white and colour service rates?
4. What happens when the equipment reaches the end of the lease?
5. What response time is available for service?
6. Can the equipment handle the business's monthly volume?
7. Are scanning, finishing and paper-handling options included?

A useful lease comparison should consider the complete operating cost rather than only the advertised monthly payment.
