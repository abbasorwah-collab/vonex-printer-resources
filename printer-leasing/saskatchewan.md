# Printer Leasing in Saskatchewan

Printer leasing can be useful for organizations that need business-class equipment without purchasing the full hardware cost upfront.

The right arrangement depends on print volume, equipment requirements, service coverage, supplies and contract terms. Saskatchewan businesses should also consider where onsite service is available and how quickly a technician can respond.

See Vonex's [Saskatchewan printer lease and rental information](https://vonex.ca/lease-rental) and [services](https://vonex.ca/services).

For organizations considering managed print, the [managed print services overview](https://vonex.ca/managed-print) explains how service, supplies and equipment management can be combined.

## Lease versus purchase

A purchase may provide more ownership flexibility, while a lease may spread equipment costs over time. The best choice depends on cash flow, expected equipment life, printing volume and service requirements.

There is no single arrangement that fits every business.
