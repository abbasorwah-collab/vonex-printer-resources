# Printer Lease vs Buy

The lease-versus-buy decision should start with the business's actual printing requirements.

Compare:

- Equipment purchase price
- Lease payments
- Expected service costs
- Toner and other supplies
- Print volume
- Contract length
- Equipment replacement cycle
- End-of-term obligations

Businesses should request a complete cost comparison before signing an equipment agreement.
