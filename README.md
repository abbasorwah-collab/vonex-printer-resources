# Canadian Printer, Copier & Office Equipment Resources

Practical resources for Canadian businesses choosing, maintaining, leasing and managing printers, copiers, scanners and related office equipment.

These guides cover printer leasing, repairs, managed print services, toner and ink, copier service, and equipment selection. The resources are written for businesses rather than as product advertisements.
